### Day-by-Day Itinerary

#### Day 1 (Dec 10, 2026): Journey from Haldwani & Arrival in Miami
* **Morning (05:30 – 11:30):** Board the *Kathgodam–New Delhi Shatabdi Express* from Haldwani to New Delhi Railway Station. Take the Delhi Airport Metro Express directly to Terminal 3.
* **Afternoon/Evening:** Board your outbound transatlantic flight to Miami International Airport (MIA). *(Due to crossing into the Eastern Time Zone [-10.5 hours], you will land in Miami on the evening of the same calendar day).*
* **Night:** 
  * Arrive at Miami (MIA), clear US Customs & Border Protection, and take a cab/rideshare to your hotel in South Beach.
  * Check into your hotel and take an evening stroll along **Ocean Drive**.
  * **Dinner:** Authentic Cuban lechón asado (roast pork) or Cuban sandwiches at a local diner like *Puerto Sagua*.

#### Day 2 (Dec 11, 2026): South Beach Sunshine & High-Energy Nightlife
* **Morning:** Head to **South Beach (Lummus Park)**. Relax on the white sand, swim in the turquoise water, and admire the iconic colorful Art Deco lifeguard towers.
* **Lunch:** Fresh seafood (stone crab claws or fried fish tacos) around South Beach.
* **Afternoon:** Stroll through the **Art Deco Historic District** along Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue, then explore the boutique shops on Lincoln Road.
* **Evening & Night:** 
  * **Dinner:** Savory Latin-Caribbean steaks or jerk chicken.
  * **Nightlife:** Experience iconic Miami nightlife. Start with live Latin music and mojitos at *Mango’s Tropical Cafe*, then hit a premier club like *LIV* or the 24-hour mega-club *E11EVEN* in downtown Miami.

#### Day 3 (Dec 12, 2026): Little Havana Culture, Bayside & Brickell Lounges
* **Morning:** Visit **Little Havana**. Walk down *Calle Ocho (SW 8th St)*, watch locals play dominoes at Domino Park, and sample Cuban pastries (empanadas, pastelitos de guayaba y carne).
* **Lunch:** Traditional Cuban lunch featuring *Ropa Vieja* (shredded beef stew) and sweet plantains at *Versailles Restaurant*.
* **Afternoon:** Head to **Bayside Marketplace** and embark on a 90-minute *Millionaire's Row Boat Cruise* across Biscayne Bay to view celebrity homes and the Miami skyline from the water.
* **Evening & Night:**
  * Explore the **Wynwood Art District** to see vibrant street murals (Wynwood Walls) and sample craft beers.
  * **Nightlife:** Sophisticated cocktails at a rooftop bar in Brickell (such as *Sugar* on the 40th floor of EAST Miami) overlooking the illuminated city skyline.

#### Day 4 (Dec 13, 2026): Mid-Beach Boardwalk & Return Journey
* **Morning:** Wake up early for sunrise over the Atlantic Ocean. Take a walk along the scenic **Miami Beach Boardwalk** and enjoy a hearty American breakfast (chicken and waffles or bacon-and-egg bagels).
* **Midday:** Check out of the hotel, complete last-minute shopping around Bayside or Lincoln Road, and transfer to Miami International Airport (MIA).
* **Afternoon:** Depart Miami on your return flight back to New Delhi (connecting back by train to Haldwani).
