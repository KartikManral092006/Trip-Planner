## Warnings

- OVER BUDGET by 52,800 INR (total 82,800 vs 30,000 budget) - even with budget hotel and trains, flights alone exceed the budget.
- Train option (Delhi-Madgaon Rajdhani 3AC) is ~32h each way - very long journeys consuming 2 of 4 days.
- Only 1 full day in Goa due to 32h train journeys each way; consider extending the trip.
- December is peak season - expect higher prices and crowds, especially around Christmas.
- Anjuna Flea Market is closed on Friday (Dec 11); Mapusa Friday Market is the substitute.
- South Goa beaches (Palolem/Agonda) are quieter but farther from North Goa nightlife; plan local transport accordingly.

## Suggestions

- Switch from flights to trains: Dehradun-Delhi hop + Delhi-Madgaon Rajdhani 3AC saves ~23,000-25,000 vs flights, but adds ~32h travel each way.
- Downgrade hotel from Park Inn Candolim (~27,000/3 nights) to Vintage Beach Resort Agonda (~7,900/3 nights) to save ~19,000.
- Skip Tito's Lane nightclub (saves 2,000) or choose a cheaper shacks/bar on the Candolim strip.
- Eat at local non-veg joints (Sai Family Restaurant, etc.) to keep food costs near the lower end of the 6,400 estimate.
- Combine train + budget hotel to fit within 30,000, accepting the long 32h train rides each way.
- Consider extending the trip to 6-7 days so the 32h train journeys don't consume most of the holiday.
